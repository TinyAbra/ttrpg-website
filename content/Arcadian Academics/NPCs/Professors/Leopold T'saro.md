A fairly recent graduate of the academy himself, Leopold now oversees the spellcraft training of students. There remains a small amount of contention from some professors with higher seniority but with the backing of the former department head, the headmaster, and the Empress, Leopold's authority goes unchallenged.  

### Teaches:
-  Spellcasting (Somatic, Kinetic, Conduit, Reactive)
-  Enchanting (Basics, Complex, Entwined)
-  History (Saints & Sages, Fables)
-  Combat Magic
