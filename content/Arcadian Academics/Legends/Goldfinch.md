
The Goldfinch legend is as much history to the Niords as it is an enchanting fireside story. It tells the tale of the First Emporer's most loyal general: A woman from a small, rural town who donned her father's armour and attended the war in his stead, advised by the split-soul sage that later became the First Empress.

### The Facts:
- Xianhua and Suiren were conscripted into the seventh prince's army in the early days of the war. Xianhua worked her way from foot soldier to general, while Suiren acted as medic and advisor. 
- Xianhua was efficient with the resources available to them, including Suiren's ability to predict sudden weather changes, ambushes, and illness. Her troops had fewer casualties and higher moral outside of direct combat - sieges were a popular strategy for them. 
- After forming a base at the former castle of Lazarus, The seventh prince's army expanded outward to the south. While Xianhua did not personally attend many of the battles, her reputation frightened many smaller entities into surrendering. 
- Several decades after the successful takeover of what now forms Niordu's borders, Xianhua led the defense against an undescribed "Army from the Sea".
- Exactly 100 years after the death of Xianhua, a rare bird often associated with her portraits made a nest above a cradle. That infant later became a famous knight and led the military against another undescribed "Army from the Sea". This trend has reoccurred every hundred years since, though the invading army is not always from the sea.  

#### The Fable:

The Goldfinch is a famously undefeatable soldier that protects Niordu from foreign attacks. Starting with Xianhua, who was supposedly never seen injured despite fighting in the vanguard of many battle, the legend continued with Rena, an archer that never missed their target; Xiran, who famously developed techniques still used by Romero's military; Ellis, whose command over large battles defended Revexi's borders from Jaskian invasion; and several others. Notably the Goldfinch is always female, but there are records implying that the seventh incarnation was a split-soul.
