"I am the master of Anthony Black, our futures are bound by a contract written in blood."

'The Master of Anthony Black' is a classic romance novel based on the legend of Anthony Black, a prophet of Asyani who lived and died in the 2nd century. 

### The Facts:
- Drake Falkenrath born in the year 135; Anthony Black born in the year 139.
- War breaks out between the Kingdom of Black & the Kingdom of Falk in y154. No definitive cause. 
- Anthony Black was among the first foreign graduates at the Imperial Academy, in the graduating class of y156.
- Drake inherits the throne in late summer y156. 
- King Desman of the Kingdom of Black, eldest son of King Karstan, commits suicide in the winter of y157. Anthony Black inherits the throne.  
- In y158, Anthony Black and and Drake Falkenrath are married. Later that year, Drake marries concubine Violet Wyndham. 
- The kingdoms of Black and Falk experience a golden era from y158 to y177
- Anthony Black is assassinated at a year-end festival, held on the 2nd day of y178. His nephew, Desman Black II, inherits the throne of the Kingdom of Black. 
- Drake Falkenrath abdicates the throne to his son in y187. He dies in year 194.

#### The Fable:

Anthony Black was blessed with the gift of prophecy. Foreseeing the destruction of both his and his enemy nation to the war started by their fathers, Anthony proposes a political marriage to the rival king, Drake Falkenrath. Since the Black royal line hasn't even extended family to propose a 'suitable' marriage with, Anthony offers himself. Within several steamy slow-burn romance chapters Anthony and Drake form a close bond, defying the advice of their court, enemies from beyond their borders, and orders from the goddess of fate. Everything comes to a head when Anthony begins to deny the prophecies, garnering the wrath of Asyani. As Anthony died, Drake challenged the goddess to break an oath of loyalty and bound his soul to Anthony's, creating the first known Twin Soul.
