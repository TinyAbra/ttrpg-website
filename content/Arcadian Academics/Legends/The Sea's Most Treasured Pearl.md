"Her skin is as pale as moonlight and her hair shines like silver. What else might she be but a goddess?"

A romantic opera fantasising about the origins of Mausevi's Queen. It began with a small theater troupe that played in market squares, but was discovered by the King who was so fond of it, he built them a theater to perform in. 

### The Facts:
- Kyllian Maurvais is an avid sailor, and frequently does tours across the coast. On one such tour a sudden squall overturned the ship and he was found on a nearby beach. He claimed to have been saved by a beautiful woman, but the sailors attested no such lady had been present. 
- A month later, Mausevi's highest ranking advisor brought a woman she had found 'at sea' whom Kyllian recognized as his saviour. 
- The two had a whirlwind romance as Kyllian sought to divorce from his arranged wife to marry his true love. The exact details of their separation was never publicized, but Kyllian was granted land on the southern border where he ruled as king, and subsequently cut ties with the kingdom of Sorvia.

#### The Legend:

Kyllian Maurvais celebrated his twenty-first birthday with a cruise along the Sorvian coast. It was a grand party with only his closest friends and his trusted crew. The ship was besieged by a sudden squall and the mast snapped in two by a flash of lightning. Kyllian Maurvais was thrown to the sea. 

He was rescued from the sea by a noble dragon who protected him on the shore until aid arrived, leaving behind only the final notes of a song and the tell-tale splash of its tail sliding beneath the waves. Kyllian told his advisors of a dream about a beautiful woman who shone like a pearl and had hair as white as snow. Most of his doctors assured him that the woman was likely a figment of his imagination to fill the gaps in his memory. 

For weeks, Kyllian pinned over this imaginary woman. He sailed along the coast day after day, hoping for just a glimpse of the woman that had saved him. Many ladies seeking power and luxury tried to seduce the king by bleaching their hair and skin, but he would not be fooled by such fakes. Kyllian's friends began to worry that if they couldn't find the maiden soon, the man would wither and die of a broken heart. 


Beneath the sea lies a kingdom of dragons and sirens, filled with glittering treasures.
