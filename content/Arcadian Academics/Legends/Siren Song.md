'Siren Song' is a tragic romance about a Siren who fell in love with a human but failed to win his heart. 


### The Facts:
 It is often considered that the Sea King grants amnesty to grief-filled souls that offer their bodies to the sea and turns them into sirens - beautiful, bewitching creatures that lure ships off course into hidden rocks and cliffs with their singing. While their origin is questionable, sirens are known to exist and dragon teeth are worn as protection against their songs. 


#### The Fable:
The Souls of those who die at sea become sirens, and they cannot return to land but for one exception: the spell of a Sea Witch. When a Siren falls in love with a human prince, she begs the sea witch to give her legs. She is told the spell will only last seven sunsets, and if she can gain the man's heart, the spell will last for as long as her husband loves her. If she fails, she will die. Foolish and eager, the siren agrees and makes her way to the surface. 

 From story to story, their ending changes. In some, the prince falls in love with another woman and the siren returns to the sea heartbroken; often she dies and turns to sea foam. In others, she wins the prince's heart with a grand gesture (typically at his wedding) and she lives happily ever after as his queen. A favourite in Jask is a story where after failing to win the prince's heart away from another, the siren is offered a knife by her sisters - if she cuts out his heart and preserves it, she can live as a human due to the vague wording of the spell. The Siren refuses to hurt her beloved and instead dies of heartbreak.
